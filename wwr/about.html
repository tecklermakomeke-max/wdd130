Nile River Explorer

Our mission is to guide adventures of all skils levels safely through trilling rapids of the Nile River, providing professional equickment, expert guides and create an unforgettable rafting experiences that connect people with nature while creating lasting memiories for families and friends.

History

Nile river was founded by two brothers in 2005 who grew up near the river. They goal was to create safe and exciting rafting adventure for everyone to enjoy.Starting with just oneraft and a small team of local guides, company has grown over the years to welcome visitors from around the world to experience the beauty and trill of the Nile River firsthand.
