History

Nile river was founded by two brothers in 2005 who grew up near the river. They goal was to create safe and exciting rafting adventure for everyone to enjoy.Starting with just oneraft and a small team of local guides, company has grown over the years to welcome visitors from around the wold.

Our history started on this blue river,surrounded by rocks and shaded trees. what started as a small local tour has grown into the region favourite rafting experience.for over the years we have been guiding families and friends showing through these same rapids.
