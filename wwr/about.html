Nile River EXplorer

At Nile River Explorer, we create safe and trilling whitewater rafting experiences for begginers and professionsl alike . we paddle through powerful rapids, enjoy the beauty of the Nile and make memories with friends and family under the guidanceof our certified rafting expects.

History

Nile River Explorer was founded in 2008 when two brothers who grow up along the Nile decided to turn their love of the river into guided rafting trips. starting with one wooden boat and handful of local clients, the company grew through the word of mouth. Today, we run daily trips for adventurers from over 20 countries, but we still use the same safety approach of our founders started with.
